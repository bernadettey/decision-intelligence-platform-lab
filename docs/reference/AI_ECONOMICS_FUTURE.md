# AI Economics Future

## Purpose

AI economics is a future capability, not an M1 implementation requirement.

M1 may reserve concepts for future analysis, but it must not design or implement a full AI economics schema.

## Reserved Conceptual Flow

```text
AI usage
    -> model
    -> tokens
    -> cost
    -> workflow
    -> business unit / cost centre
    -> accepted outcome
    -> business benefit
```

## Future Analysis

Later milestones may analyze:

- Cost per accepted investigation.
- Cost per workflow.
- Analyst hours saved.
- Economic benefit.
- ROI.
- Marginal cost.
- Marginal benefit.
- Resource allocation / optimisation.

## M1 Boundary

Do not allow AI economics to expand M1 scope.

M1 should not implement:

- Token-cost accounting.
- AI ROI calculations.
- Marginal benefit analysis.
- Marginal cost analysis.
- Resource allocation or optimisation logic.
